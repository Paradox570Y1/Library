- Brokerage - broker's fee for executing your trade.
	- Flat brokerage - Whether you trade ₹5,000 or ₹50 lakh, you still pay ₹20.
		- `Zerodha`, `Angel One`, `Upstox`
	- Percentage brokerage - 0.30% of trade value, for ₹10 lakh it will be 3000.
		- `ICICI Direct`, `Kotak Securities`, `IIFL Securities`, `Motilal Oswal`
- Securities Transaction Tax (STT)
	- Paid to Government of India
	- Exactly like income tax
	- Every broker (Zerodha, Angel One, Groww, Dhan, Upstox, ICICI Direct, etc.) charges the **same STT** because it is fixed by law.
	- You cannot negotiate it
	- STT rates depend on the *type of trade* and have changed over time (change over time)

| Trade Type                             |   STT Rate | Charged On                         | Paid By        |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------: | ---------------------------------- | -------------- |
| **Equity Delivery – Buy**              |  **0.10%** | Transaction value                  | Buyer          |
| **Equity Delivery – Sell**             |  **0.10%** | Transaction value                  | Seller         |
| **Equity Intraday (Non-delivery)**     | **0.025%** | Sell transaction value             | Seller         |
| **Equity Futures**                     |  **0.05%** | Sell transaction value             | Seller         |
| **Equity Options (Sell)**              |  **0.15%** | Option premium                     | Seller         |
| **Equity Options (Exercised)**         |  **0.15%** | Intrinsic value (settlement value) | Buyer          |
| **Equity-Oriented Mutual Fund (Sell)** | **0.001%** | Transaction value                  | Seller         |
| **Units of Business Trust (Delivery)** |  **0.10%** | Buy & Sell transaction value       | Buyer & Seller |


- Stamp Duty
	- Another government charge.
	- Legal recognition of financial transactions.
	- For stocks: generally charged on the **buy side only**, not on the sell side
- Exchange Transaction Charges
	- Paid to: NSE, BSE, MCX
	- They maintain:
		- trading servers
		- matching engines
		- disaster recovery systems
		- market infrastructure
- SEBI Turnover Charges
	- Funding market regulation and oversight.
	- It is extremely small but applies across trades.
- GST
	- It is often misunderstood.
	- It is not paid on investment itself instead is charged on the services you consume.
		- brokerage,
		- exchange transaction charges,
		- SEBI charges
- DP Charges
	- DP stands for Depository Participant
	- Examples: Angel One, Zerodha, Groww
	- Usually associated with when you sell delivery shares from your demat account.
	- Not generally on intraday trades because no demat movement occurs.
- Annual Maintenance Charge (AMC)
	- Maintaining your DMAT account costs money
	- Many brokers charge ₹0 , ₹300, ₹500 etc. Some wave it for one year
- Margin Interest
	- With ₹20,000 of your own funds, you can buy shares worth ₹1 lakh by borrowing the remaining ₹80,000 from your broker.
	- Interest starts accruing on the borrowed ₹80,000 immediately. Rates vary by broker and can become expensive if you hold the position for several weeks or months
- Margin Penalty
	- If you need ₹1,00,000 to place a trade but have only ₹70,000, the broker may still allow the trade depending on its margin policy.
	- If the exchange later identifies a margin shortfall, a penalty may be imposed. Maintaining sufficient margin helps avoid these penalties.
- Auto Square-off Charges
	- If you don't close your intraday position before the market closes (typically by the broker's cut-off time before 3:30 PM), the broker automatically closes it on your behalf.
	- Most brokers levy an additional **auto square-off fee** for forcibly closing the intraday position.
- Call & Trade Charges
	- Place orders over the phone if you can't access the trading platform.
	- Most brokers charge **₹20–₹100 per order** for this service.
- Pledge Charges
	- Use your shares as collateral to get trading margin.
	- Usually **₹20–₹50 per pledge request**.
- Dishonour Charges
	- if the broker cannot debit your bank account due to insufficient balance.
	- A penalty may apply for each failed debit attempt.
- Account Closure Charges
	- Usually free sometimes not, always verify
- Account Opening Charges
	- Mostly free
- Research Subscription
	- Research Subscription
- Platform Charges
	- Older brokers charged for
		- desktop terminal
		- market data
		- advanced software
