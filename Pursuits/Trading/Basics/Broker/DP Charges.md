###### **DP (Depository Participant) charge** is:

- **₹20 + 18% GST = ₹23.60** **per scrip (ISIN), per transaction**.

A few important points:

- ✅ Charged **only when you sell delivery shares** from your Demat account.
- ❌ **Not charged** on:
    - Buying delivery shares
    - Intraday trades
    - Futures & Options (F&O)
- It is charged **per scrip per transaction**, not based on the number of shares.

##### Examples

- Sell **100 shares of TCS** in one transaction → **₹23.60**
- Sell **1,000 shares of TCS** in one transaction → **₹23.60**
- Sell **TCS and Infosys** in the same transaction/day → **₹47.20** (₹23.60 × 2)
