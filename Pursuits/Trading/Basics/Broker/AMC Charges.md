###### **AMC (Annual Maintenance Charges)** for an Angel One Demat account:
- **First year:** **₹0** (free)
- **From the second year:**
    - **Non-BSDA Demat account:** **₹60 + GST per quarter** (₹240 + GST per year), charged after your first trade in that quarter.
    - **BSDA (Basic Services Demat Account):**
        - Holdings **below ₹4 lakh:** **₹0 AMC**
        - Holdings **₹4 lakh to ₹10 lakh:** **₹100 + GST per year**

**Eligibility for BSDA:**  
Your account is generally eligible for BSDA if:

- It's your **only Demat account** across all brokers (under your PAN).
- You're the **sole or first holder**.
- Your total Demat holdings are **below ₹10 lakh**.
