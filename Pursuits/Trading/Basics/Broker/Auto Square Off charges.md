##### Angel One's **Auto Square-off charge** is:

- **₹20 per executed order** when Angel One automatically squares off your open intraday position.

### Example

- You buy 100 shares of Reliance as an **intraday** trade.
- You forget to sell them before the auto square-off time.
- Angel One automatically sells them.
- **Auto square-off charge:** ₹20 (plus the normal brokerage and statutory charges applicable to that trade).
