Here's an easy analogy:

- **Bank account** → stores your money.
- **Demat account (with CDSL/NSDL)** → stores your shares, ETFs, bonds, mutual fund units, and other securities electronically.


**CDSL** stands for **Central Depository Services (India) Limited**. It is one of India's two central securities depositories, the other being National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL).
